# Arrange your workbench

Panels can be dragged, stacked as tabs, split, maximized (□) and closed.

- **Layout ▾** switches presets: *Table focus*, *Hex focus (classic)*, *Structure browser*,
  *Investigate & annotate*, *Overview* — plus scenario layouts shipped with your project.
- **Reset** restores the preset; **Save layout as…** keeps your arrangement.
- **NVM: Workbench: Export Layout as Scenario File…** writes a `*.nvmworkbench.json`
  your colleagues get automatically for dumps in the same folder.
- Prefer the old single hex view? Set `nvmstudio.nvm.layoutMode` to `classic`.
